Persian speech listening test / آزمون شنیداری گفتار فارسی

About 15 minutes, with headphones in a quiet room. Listen to each clip at least once; replay as often as you like. There are no right voices to prefer — answer by ear. / لطفاً با هدفون و در محیط آرام گوش دهید.

Listener number:

Part 1 — What kind of sentence is it? How natural is it?

The text is shown without its final punctuation. Decide from the voice whether it is a statement, a question, or an exclamation / surprise, then rate how natural the voice sounds (1 = very unnatural, 5 = completely natural).

Part 2 — Compare A and B

The same text, two versions. Which sounds more natural (−3 = A much better, 0 = equal, +3 = B much better)? Which conveys the punctuation better — questions like questions, exclamations and surprise like them, a text that is not monotone?

Part 3 — Short replies: is every word clear?

Rate how clearly you understand every word (1 = not at all, 5 = every word clear) and tick if a word is missing, wrong or garbled.
